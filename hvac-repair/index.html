Warning Signs for HVAC Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. These are the signals our technicians see most often when a problem is underway:

  • Short cycling, where the system starts and stops without ever reaching the setpoint
  • Unfamiliar sounds from the equipment, like squealing, grinding, or banging
  • An outside unit that buzzes but will not start
  • Weak airflow from the supply registers throughout the house
  • A breaker that trips whenever the system tries to run
  • Air at the registers that is the wrong temperature for the mode you selected
  • Rising utility costs even though the house feels worse
  • Water around the indoor unit from a clogged condensate drain

How to Prevent HVAC Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Give the condenser room to breathe by keeping plants, leaves, and clippings away from it
  • Run a vinegar flush through the condensate line each spring to prevent clogs and float switch trips
  • Swap in a fresh filter every one to three months; a clogged one starves the whole system of air
  • Give the system a rest during extreme weather by setting the thermostat a few degrees closer to outdoors
  • Have the system professionally serviced twice a year, once before summer and once before winter
  • Do not ignore new sounds; catching a noisy motor before it locks up saves real money
  • Avoid closing or covering registers; the system is designed to move air through all of them
