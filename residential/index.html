Upfront Pricing

Honest, Upfront Pricing

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. York HVAC offers honest, upfront pricing across York, handling everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a careful inspection of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a plain-English recommendation with the price spelled out before any work starts.

Most HVAC failures give quiet warnings long before the system quits. Watch for rising utility bills, weak airflow at the registers, frost on the copper lines, or rooms that never catch up to the thermostat. Behind those symptoms are usually slow refrigerant leaks, worn blower bearings, or a capacitor on its way out. A quick inspection at the first sign often costs far less than an emergency replacement.

How old is your system? Once an air conditioner or furnace passes the 12-year mark, it's worth thinking about the next one before the current one forces the issue. Emergency replacements happen on the worst days, in heat waves and cold snaps, when options are few and everyone is uncomfortable. Our technicians know how the York climate affects equipment and will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is running on borrowed time.

Preventive Care

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

Routine maintenance is the most reliable way to keep your home comfortable year-round. Regular service prevents failures before they start, keeps coils and burners clean, and catches small faults while they're still cheap to fix. At York HVAC, every plan is built around your home's specific equipment, with scheduled visits that cover coil cleaning, refrigerant charge verification, capacitor and contactor testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, condensate drain clearing, filter replacement, and a static pressure check on the ductwork.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and the plan is tailored to your equipment's age, your household's comfort demands, and your local climate.

Think of maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers what you pay to run the system while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most families, a plan costs far less than replacing the system ahead of schedule.

Right-Sized Systems

The Right Size System for Your Home

Getting the size right matters more than most homeowners realize. Before quoting new equipment, York HVAC measures the details that determine your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. That gives you a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, controls humidity, and keeps every room at the temperature you set.

We walk you through the options for every home, especially growing families with rising comfort demands. A single-stage system that served a small household rarely satisfies a finished basement and two added bedrooms, and a modern two-stage or variable-speed heat pump can hold tighter temperatures while cutting energy use significantly. If you're weighing furnace-and-AC against a heat pump, or ducted against ductless, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: SEER2 efficiency ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting requirements, and upfront installation work, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Frequent Failures

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in York

No cooling, a furnace that refuses to light, ice on the indoor coil, leaking refrigerant: every one of these problems has its own cause and calls for its own repair. York HVAC diagnoses the exact fault before replacing anything, whether it's a condenser that hums without starting, a short-cycling furnace, a thermostat that loses its settings, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split throwing error codes.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A bad capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a blocked condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
